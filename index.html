💡 What Is open-spark-jev?

open-spark-jev is a free, open-source tool that brings powerful AI decision-making to your own computer. It's inspired by TypeSafe's Jev and System One, two respected approaches for building smart, reliable decision systems. We've combined those ideas with the modern capabilities of Qwen3, a leading language model, to create something truly useful for everyday users.

Think of it as having a brilliant advisor sitting inside your PC. It can help you analyze situations, weigh options, and reach better conclusions - all without sending your data anywhere. Everything runs locally, which means your information stays private and works even without an internet connection.

Whether you're a small business owner trying to optimize workflows, a student researching complex topics, or just a curious person who wants to explore what local AI can do, open-spark-jev is built for you.
